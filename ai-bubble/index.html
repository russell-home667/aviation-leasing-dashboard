AI Bubble Monitor is a monitoring framework, not an investment recommendation. Bubble Score measures excess; Breakdown Score measures unwind confirmation. Breakdown severity bands are historically calibrated with a 1999-present proxy backtest; Bubble Score remains a transparent modern-era heuristic because comparable 2000-era CapEx/cloud/GPU data do not exist. All displayed timestamps use Beijing Time (UTC+8).
